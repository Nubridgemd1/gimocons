This Facility Transportation Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of the Effective Date below, by and between GIMO Consulting Services (“GIMO”), a non‑emergency medical transportation provider, and the facility identified in the signature block (the “Facility”). GIMO and the Facility are each a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

  1. 1. Services. GIMO will provide non‑emergency medical transportation (“NEMT”) for the Facility’s residents, patients, and clients (“Riders”), including ambulatory, wheelchair‑accessible, and stretcher transport to and from medical appointments, dialysis and recurring treatment, hospital discharge, day programs, and similar destinations, on the schedule requested and accepted under this Agreement.
  2. 2. Scheduling & Advance Notice. The Facility will request trips through GIMO’s facility portal, by phone, or by email. All trips must be scheduled at least twenty‑four (24) hours in advance, except in extenuating circumstances (such as an unplanned hospital discharge or urgent same‑day medical need), which GIMO will make reasonable efforts to accommodate subject to vehicle and driver availability. Standing/recurring trips (e.g., dialysis) may be set once and repeated. GIMO will confirm each trip and provide the assigned driver and pickup details.
  3. 3. Facility Obligations. The Facility will: (a) request trips with accurate Rider, pickup, destination, date/time, and mobility information; (b) ensure Riders are ready at the scheduled pickup time; (c) disclose any mobility needs, oxygen, or special assistance in advance; (d) provide any required authorizations for covered trips; (e) designate authorized personnel to request and approve trips; and (f) pay for services as set out in Section 5.
  4. 4. GIMO Obligations. GIMO will: (a) provide clean, insured, and regularly inspected vehicles; (b) use trained, background‑checked, drug‑screened drivers; (c) provide door‑to‑door / door‑through‑door assistance as appropriate; (d) secure wheelchairs and stretchers properly; (e) make reasonable efforts to arrive on time and communicate delays; (f) treat Rider information as confidential and handle it consistent with applicable privacy laws; and (g) confirm each requested trip with driver and pickup details.
  5. 5. Fees & Payment. Services are billed at GIMO’s standard rates or at facility contract rates agreed in writing (currently mileage‑based at $3.50/mile counted both ways on round trips, plus handling where applicable: wheelchair +$75, stretcher +$45; ambulatory has no surcharge). Payment is by cash or ACH bank transfer (no card‑processing fees), on a per‑trip basis or on a monthly invoice as agreed. Invoices are due within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date unless otherwise agreed.
  6. 6. Cancellations & No‑Shows. The Facility should cancel or change a trip as early as possible. Cancellations with less than two (2) hours’ notice, and Rider no‑shows at pickup, may be billed as a trip, as reasonably determined by GIMO.
  7. 7. Non‑Emergency Service; Safety & Compliance. GIMO provides non‑emergency transportation only and is not an ambulance or emergency medical service; in a medical emergency the Facility will call 911. GIMO maintains commercial auto and general liability insurance and complies with applicable federal, state, and local transportation and safety requirements.
  8. 8. Term & Termination. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated. Either Party may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days’ written notice, or immediately for material breach that is not cured within ten (10) days of written notice. Trips already scheduled will be honored or settled in good faith.
  9. 9. Limitation of Liability & Indemnification. Except for gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Each Party will indemnify the other against third‑party claims arising from its own negligence or breach of this Agreement, subject to available insurance.
  10. 10. Independent Contractor. GIMO is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship between the Parties.
  11. 11. Entire Agreement; Amendments. This Agreement, together with any written facility rate schedule, is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions. It may be amended only in writing signed (including electronically) by both Parties.
  12. 12. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict‑of‑laws rules.
